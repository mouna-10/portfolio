01

Catching machine drift before it stops a line

Airbus Atlantic · Nantes

Problem
More than 100 sensor variables streaming off aeronautical production systems, with faults only becoming visible once they had already interrupted production.
Approach
Structured the multivariate time series end to end: cleaning, resampling, missing-value handling and feature engineering. Built anomaly detection models on top to surface drift ahead of failure, then industrialised the whole path in Python so it ran unattended, with quality and lineage controls on every input.
Result
Analytical output integrated into the operational maintenance process rather than sitting in a notebook.
PythonTime seriesAnomaly detectionFeature engineeringData quality
02

Moving a reporting estate off SAP BusinessObjects

CETIH · Machecoul

Problem
Data integration ran on SAP BusinessObjects Data Services and had to migrate to Talend without losing the reporting the business depended on daily.
Approach
Rebuilt the ETL flows in Talend, then industrialised and optimised the integration and transformation layer. Remodelled the data for several business domains and rebuilt the Power BI dashboards on top of the new pipeline.
Result
Business teams kept their dashboards through the migration, on a stack the company could maintain.
TalendSAP BODSPostgreSQLPower BIData modelling
03

A retrieval assistant for administrative correspondence

Sénégal Numérique · Dakar

Problem
Government departments produce a high volume of administrative letters that staff were summarising by hand, one at a time.
Approach
Built a retrieval-augmented generation pipeline on Qwen: document ingestion, indexing, semantic search across the corpus, and prompt engineering tuned for summary quality. Took it through to production rather than stopping at a prototype.
Result
Summarisation of incoming correspondence handled by the system instead of by hand.
